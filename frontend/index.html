How it works

LoanSight passes your inputs to a trained Random Forest classification model and reports what the model returns.

  1. Borrower information

    You enter eleven details about the borrower, the loan and the credit file. Each one is checked in the browser before anything is sent.

  2. Machine learning analysis

    The details go to a FastAPI service. A Random Forest, an ensemble of many decision trees, scores the application.

  3. Risk assessment result

    The service returns a score and a classification. The score is compared with a 0.40 threshold to label the borrower as low risk or risky.

About LoanSight

LoanSight is a machine learning-based loan risk assessment application designed to help understand borrower risk through structured financial information.

  • A decision aid. It supports human review and does not replace it.
  • An uncalibrated score. Scores show relative risk and are not exact odds of default.
  • Dependent on training data. Results reflect the data the model learned from.
